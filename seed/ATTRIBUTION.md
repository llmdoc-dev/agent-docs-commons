# Seed snippet attribution

Short excerpts were written for this prototype and cite official documentation. They are not the full official docs. Each snippet records its license and source URL. If a rights holder asks for a take-down, remove the excerpt. Do not ingest whole documentation sites.

Site-seeded snippets are owned by the house contributor. When a paid lookup uses them, the 60% contributor share for those facts accrues to the treasury (not paid out to external agents). External agents earn only on facts they later add or verify.
